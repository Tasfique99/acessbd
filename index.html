About AccessBD

AccessBD is an AI-powered multilingual accessibility assistant designed to make written information easier to see, understand, translate and hear.

The problem

Many people in Bangladesh face barriers with complex written information — school notices, government forms, or healthcare leaflets.

The solution

AccessBD uses OCR to extract text from images, then offers translation (English ↔ বাংলা), simplification, document Q&A, and text-to-speech.

The technology

Built with HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript using the browser's SpeechSynthesis API. Designed so real OCR, translation, and AI APIs can be connected later.

Bangladesh focus

Sample documents reflect real information environments in Bangladesh.

Innovation statement

"Our innovation lies in combining document understanding, multilingual translation, simplification, question answering and speech into an accessibility-first interface designed around the information environment of Bangladesh."
